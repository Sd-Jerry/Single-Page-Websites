Section 01

Overview

Welcome to BL Events ("we", "our", "us"). We are an event management company headquartered at 2nd Floor, Shree Complex, Kalawad Road, Rajkot — 360005, Gujarat, India, operating under the trade name BL Events.

This Privacy Policy applies to all information collected through our website blevents.in, our inquiry forms, our social media pages, and any related services we provide (collectively, the "Services").

By using our website or submitting an inquiry, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of our Services and contact us to discuss your concerns.

This policy has been prepared in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 of India.

Section 02

Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to our clients and to respond to your event inquiries. The types of information we collect include:

Personal Information

Full name, email address, phone number, WhatsApp number, and city/location when you fill out our inquiry or contact forms.

Event Details

Event type, event date, venue preferences, expected guest count, budget range, and specific service requirements you provide.

Usage Data

Browser type, IP address, pages visited, time spent on pages, referring URLs, and device type — collected automatically when you visit our site.

Communication Records

Records of your correspondence with us via email, WhatsApp, phone, or social media — including quotes, proposals, and feedback.

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as financial account numbers, credit/debit card details, Aadhaar numbers, PAN numbers, or health information through our website. Any payment processing is handled through secure third-party payment gateways.

Section 03

How We Use Your Data

The information we collect is used strictly for legitimate business purposes. We use your personal data to:

Process your event inquiry — to understand your requirements and prepare a customised event proposal tailored to your vision and budget.

Communicate with you — to follow up on your inquiry via call, email, or WhatsApp and provide you with timely updates throughout the planning process.

Deliver our services — to plan, coordinate, and execute your event with our team of professionals and trusted vendor network.

Send service updates — to notify you of important changes, booking confirmations, event day details, and post-event delivery timelines.

Improve our website and services — to analyse usage data and understand how visitors interact with our website so we can improve the user experience.

Send marketing communications — with your explicit consent, to share event tips, trend updates, and special offers via our newsletter. You may unsubscribe at any time.

Legal and compliance purposes — to comply with applicable Indian laws, respond to legal requests, and protect our rights and the rights of our clients.

We will never use your personal information for any purpose beyond what is described in this policy without your explicit consent. Marketing communications are opt-in only and you can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us directly.

Section 04

Data Sharing & Disclosure

BL Events does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

Trusted Vendor Partners — We may share relevant event details (your name, event date, location, and requirements) with our pre-vetted vendors — decorators, caterers, photographers, AV specialists — strictly for the purpose of executing your event. These vendors are contractually bound to keep your information confidential.

Service Providers — We use trusted third-party tools to operate our website and communications, including email service providers (EmailJS), website hosting, and analytics platforms (Google Analytics). These providers process data on our behalf and are bound by appropriate data protection agreements.

Legal Requirements — We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government authority, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of BL Events, our clients, or the public.

Business Transfer — In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of business assets, your information may be transferred. We will notify you via email and/or a prominent notice on our website before your data is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

Section 05

Cookies Policy

Our website uses cookies — small text files stored on your device — to improve your browsing experience, analyse website traffic, and personalise content. By continuing to use our website, you consent to our use of cookies.

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Essential Required for the website to function properly — enables navigation, form submissions, and session management. Session
Functional Remembers your preferences (e.g. language, form data) to provide a more personalised experience on return visits. 30 days
Analytics Google Analytics cookies that help us understand how visitors interact with our website — pages visited, time on site, traffic sources. 2 years

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies. However, please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website. To opt out of Google Analytics tracking specifically, you can use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Section 06

Data Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously and implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

SSL Encryption

All data transmitted between your browser and our website is encrypted using SSL/TLS technology (HTTPS).

Access Control

Access to your personal data is restricted to authorised BL Events personnel who need it to perform their duties.

Secure Storage

Client data is stored on secure, access-controlled systems. We do not store payment card details on our servers.

Breach Notification

In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting your rights, we will notify you without undue delay as required by applicable law.

Please note that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security. We encourage you to use strong passwords and to be careful about what information you share online.

Section 07

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy and to comply with our legal obligations.

Inquiry and consultation records — Retained for up to 12 months from the date of your last interaction, to allow us to follow up on pending proposals and improve our service.

Active client records — Retained for the duration of our service engagement plus 3 years after the event, to handle any post-event queries, deliverables, or disputes.

Financial and billing records — Retained for 7 years as required under Indian tax and accounting laws (Income Tax Act, GST regulations).

Newsletter subscribers — Retained until you unsubscribe. We honour unsubscribe requests within 5 business days.

When your data is no longer required, we securely delete or anonymise it in accordance with our internal data disposal procedures.

Section 08

Your Rights

As a user of our website and a client of BL Events, you have the following rights with respect to your personal data. You may exercise these rights at any time by contacting us at hello@blevents.in.

Right to Access

Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you at any time.

Right to Correction

Request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete information we hold.

Right to Deletion

Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.

Right to Object

Object to processing of your data for marketing purposes at any time.

Right to Portability

Receive your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format.

Unsubscribe

Opt out of marketing emails at any time via the unsubscribe link or by emailing us.

We will respond to all legitimate requests within 30 days. Requests can be submitted by emailing hello@blevents.in with the subject line "Privacy Request". We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests to protect your security.

Section 10

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at children under the age of 18 years. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us immediately at hello@blevents.in and we will promptly delete such information from our records.

While we plan and execute events that involve children (such as birthday parties and family celebrations), all inquiries and bookings must be made by an adult (18 years or older). Parents or guardians must provide consent on behalf of any minors involved in event-related photography or videography.

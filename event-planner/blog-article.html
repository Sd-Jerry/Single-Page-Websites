10 Wedding Décor Trends That Will Define Celebrations in 2025

Wedding décor in Gujarat has evolved dramatically over the past decade. The era of simple flower arrangements and basic stage lighting is long gone. Today's couples want their wedding to feel like a curated experience — something their guests have never seen before and will talk about for years. At BL Events, we've had the privilege of designing over 80 weddings across Saurashtra and Gujarat, and here are the top trends defining 2025.

1. Floral Ceiling Canopies

Overhead floral installations have taken over Gujarat's wedding mandaps and reception halls. Think thousands of fresh marigolds, roses, and orchids suspended from the ceiling, creating a living, breathing canopy that glows beautifully under warm lighting. The effect is dramatic, fragrant, and completely unforgettable. Couples are choosing colour palettes that match their outfits — deep burgundy and ivory for Gujarati brides in lehenga, or orange and white for traditional saree ceremonies.

2. LED Mandap Structures

The traditional wooden mandap is getting a luminous upgrade. Couples are now choosing mandap structures embedded with warm LED strips and backlit acrylic panels that create a soft, cinematic glow. When paired with natural floral accents, the result is breathtaking — traditional at its core, modern in execution. This trend works especially well for evening ceremonies where the LED glow becomes the focal point of every photograph.

3. Personalized Welcome Gates

The first impression matters enormously. In 2025, couples are investing heavily in custom welcome gates — tall floral arches, neon name signs, and thematic entrance installations that immediately set the mood. We've designed gates for weddings that featured floating floral spheres, cascading greenery walls, and illuminated name displays in the couple's chosen font. Guests begin photographing before they even step inside.

4. Earthy, Organic Colour Palettes

Gone are the days of harsh reds and electric blues. The dominant palette in 2025 is earthy and organic — terracotta, sage green, dusty rose, warm ivory, and burnt gold. These colours photograph beautifully, feel emotionally warm, and work harmoniously with both traditional Gujarati décor elements and contemporary minimalist setups.

5. Micro-Green Tablescapes

Reception table décor has gone botanical. Instead of towering flower arrangements that block guests from seeing each other, couples are choosing low, lush tablescapes with micro-greens, herbs, seasonal flowers, and candles at varying heights. The result is an intimate, garden-like atmosphere that encourages conversation and looks stunning in overhead photographs.

The most memorable weddings are the ones where the décor tells the couple's story. Every element should feel intentional, not just decorative.

6. Candlelit Ceremony Aisles

For evening ceremonies, we're seeing a huge move toward candlelit aisles — dozens of tall pillar candles in glass hurricane vases lining the path to the mandap. Paired with rose petals and low floral arrangements, the effect is deeply romantic and completely cinematic. Modern flameless LED candle alternatives mean this look is now possible even for outdoor venues with wind.

7. The Statement Backdrop

Every couple wants that one wall — the statement backdrop — that defines their wedding in photographs. In 2025, this is moving away from generic flower walls toward bespoke creations: moss walls with embedded neon signs, suspended dried pampas grass installations, geometric metal frames draped in fabric, or even custom painted murals depicting the couple's love story.

8. Sustainable Décor Choices

Environmental consciousness is influencing wedding décor in meaningful ways. More couples are requesting locally sourced flowers, potted plants that guests can take home, biodegradable confetti, and décor elements that can be repurposed or composted after the event. BL Events actively partners with vendors who follow sustainable practices — and couples are increasingly requesting this as a must-have.

9. Personalised Scent Experiences

One of the most innovative 2025 trends is designing events around a signature scent. Couples are working with us to create a specific fragrance experience — sandalwood and rose at the mandap, fresh jasmine at the reception, light citrus at the dining area. Scent is one of the most powerful triggers of memory, and couples want their guests to forever associate that fragrance with their wedding day.

10. The Intimate Grand Finale

The wedding sendoff — traditionally a quick farewell — has become a carefully orchestrated moment in 2025. Sparkler tunnels, firefly lantern releases, bubble walls, and choreographed drone light shows are becoming common sendoff moments. When captured on video, these create the kind of footage that goes viral on Instagram and YouTube, giving couples a beautiful end to their wedding story.

BL Events Insight

The best wedding décor doesn't just look beautiful — it tells your story. Before choosing any trend, ask yourself: does this feel like us? The couples who are happiest with their wedding décor are the ones who chose authenticity over aesthetics alone.

20 Stunning Haldi Ceremony Decoration Ideas for Your Gujarat Wedding

The haldi ceremony is one of the most joyful, vibrant, and emotionally rich moments in any Gujarati wedding. It's the function where the formal wedding week becomes a family affair — full of colour, laughter, music, and the golden glow of turmeric. And increasingly, couples are treating the haldi function as a standalone event that deserves as much creative attention as the reception itself.

At BL Events, we've designed haldi setups ranging from intimate garden arrangements to elaborate open-air celebrations with over 200 family members. Here are 20 décor ideas we return to again and again because they never fail to delight.

Setting the Foundation

1
Marigold-draped sitting stage — A low wooden platform draped entirely in orange and yellow marigold garlands creates an instantly festive and photogenic backdrop for the couple.
2
Banana leaf and flower floor seating — Traditional floor seating surrounded by fresh banana leaves, marigold carpets, and terracotta lamps creates a beautifully rustic atmosphere.
3
Sunflower canopy overhead — A ceiling installation of sunflowers and yellow chrysanthemums creates a warm, golden canopy that photographs stunningly in natural light.
4
Hand-painted earthen pot centrepieces — Terracotta pots painted with traditional Gujarati motifs, filled with marigolds and daisies, placed at every table or seating cluster.
5
Yellow and orange fabric draping — Sheer fabric in turmeric yellows and saffron oranges draped from poles and trees creates a warm, tent-like atmosphere for outdoor ceremonies.

Colour and Mood

The haldi function's colour palette should be unapologetically bold. Deep yellows, bright oranges, vibrant greens, and pops of fuchsia all work beautifully together. Don't be afraid of colour saturation — haldi photographs look incredible in richly coloured settings, and the turmeric itself adds beautiful natural golden tones to every image.

6
Phool ki chadar entrance — A human flower shower entrance where family members shower the couple with petals as they walk in is one of the most loved and memorable moments.
7
Neon "Haldi Wala" sign — A custom neon sign in the couple's wedding colour adds a modern touch while creating an incredible photo backdrop.
8
Haldi shot station — A dedicated photobooth area with props like turmeric bowls, flower garlands, and hand-painted signs creates memories guests keep forever.
The haldi ceremony is the most authentic, unposed moment of the entire wedding week. Your décor should support that spirit — joyful, warm, and full of colour.

Details That Elevate the Experience

The difference between a good haldi ceremony and an extraordinary one often comes down to the small details that guests notice without consciously identifying. Custom painted haldi thalis with the couple's name. Handwoven return favours in fabric bags with turmeric sachets. Fresh flower crowns for the bride and sisters. A live dhol player who begins the music as the ceremony starts. These moments layer together to create an experience that feels completely personal.

BL Events Insight

Don't over-plan the haldi ceremony. The best moments are the unscripted ones — the grandmother applying turmeric with trembling, loving hands, the cousins erupting into impromptu dance. Your décor sets the stage; the family writes the story.

How to Choose the Perfect Wedding Venue in Rajkot & Saurashtra

The venue is arguably the single most important decision in wedding planning. It determines your guest capacity, your décor possibilities, your catering options, your photography opportunities, and the overall mood of your entire celebration. Get it right and everything else becomes easier. Get it wrong and even the most beautiful décor and finest food can't compensate.

Having organized hundreds of weddings across Rajkot, Jamnagar, Junagadh, Bhavnagar, and Surat, BL Events has worked with virtually every type of venue in Saurashtra. Here's our comprehensive guide to making the right choice.

Step 1 — Know Your Numbers First

Before you look at a single venue, have a firm sense of your guest list. The golden rule: your venue should comfortably accommodate 10–15% more than your expected headcount. Crowds feel festive; cramped guests do not. If you're expecting 400 guests, look for venues that seat 440–460 comfortably. Consider that different functions may have different headcounts — your haldi may have 150 people while your reception draws 600.

Step 2 — Venue Types in Saurashtra

1
Banquet Halls — Climate-controlled, reliable, and available year-round. Best for large city weddings. Key consideration: assess the natural light, ceiling height, and whether you can hang floral installations from the ceiling.
2
Garden / Farmhouse Venues — Ideal for October–March weddings. Stunning for photography and creating an organic, open atmosphere. Key consideration: always have a weather backup plan — even winter evenings in Gujarat can turn cold.
3
Hotel Venues — Convenient for guests travelling from out of town. In-house catering, accommodation, and coordination simplify logistics considerably. Key consideration: hotel event staff may not align with your external décor team's schedule — confirm this early.
4
Heritage & Palace Properties — Available near Rajkot, Jamnagar, and Gondal for destination-style weddings. Architecturally stunning, but require more logistical planning around catering access and guest transport.

The 8 Questions to Ask Every Venue

1
What is the maximum seated capacity and maximum standing reception capacity?
2
Is the catering in-house only, or can we bring our own caterer? What are the rates?
3
What are the setup and teardown timing windows for our team?
4
Is there a sound restriction curfew, and what is the permitted decibel level?
5
How many functions can be held simultaneously in adjacent areas?
6
What is the parking capacity, and is valet service available?
7
What is the cancellation and date-change policy?
8
Is there a backup generator, and what are the power load limits for DJ and lighting?
BL Events Insight

Visit every shortlisted venue at the same time of day as your planned function. A venue that looks stunning at noon may feel completely different at sunset. Bring your event planner to the venue visit — we ask questions couples often don't think to ask until it's too late.

How to Plan a Corporate Event That Leaves a Lasting Impression

A corporate event is one of the most powerful communication tools available to any business. Whether it's an annual conference, a client appreciation dinner, a product launch, or an employee recognition night, the way you execute your event tells your stakeholders exactly what your brand stands for. A poorly planned corporate event communicates exactly the wrong message — that you don't pay attention to detail, that you don't value your guests' time, and that you don't take yourself seriously.

Conversely, a masterfully executed corporate event can transform relationships, accelerate deals, boost employee morale, and generate significant media coverage. BL Events has organized over 60 corporate events across Gujarat. Here is our definitive framework for getting it right.

Define Your Purpose Before Your Budget

The single most common mistake companies make is discussing budget before defining purpose. Every decision — venue, format, entertainment, catering, AV — flows from the purpose. Are you trying to close deals with key clients? Celebrate employee achievement? Launch a new product to media and industry partners? Generate social media content? Onboard new team members? Each purpose demands a completely different event design.

The 6 Pillars of a Successful Corporate Event

1
Audience-First Design — Design every aspect of the event from the perspective of your attendee. What do they need to feel, know, or do as a result of this event? Start with that outcome and work backward.
2
Precision Timing — Corporate attendees have limited time and high expectations. A well-paced agenda with clear transitions, no dead time, and efficient registration processes signals competence and respect.
3
Technical Excellence — Nothing destroys a corporate event faster than poor AV. Invest in professional sound, reliable microphone systems, seamless presentation setup, and adequate lighting. Test everything twice.
4
Purposeful Networking Zones — Create structured opportunities for meaningful connections, not just a reception where people stand awkwardly. Themed conversation areas, curated seating arrangements, and facilitated introductions all help.
5
Brand Consistency — Every element — from the invitation to the stage backdrop to the menu cards — should reflect your brand's visual identity and tone of voice. Inconsistency dilutes your brand message.
6
Post-Event Follow Through — The event isn't over when guests leave. A professional follow-up — thank you notes, photo galleries, media coverage, and key action items — extends the event's impact significantly.
A great corporate event is not about impressing people. It's about giving them an experience that makes them feel valued, connected, and genuinely excited about what comes next.
BL Events Insight

The most underutilized tool in corporate event planning is a detailed run-of-show document. This minute-by-minute schedule, shared with every vendor and team member, is what separates a professionally executed event from an improvised one. We create these for every corporate event we manage.

How to Plan a Memorable Corporate Award Night — The BL Events Way

An award night is one of the most emotionally significant corporate events an organization can host. It's the evening when individual contributions are publicly recognized, when the entire organization comes together to celebrate excellence, and when leadership communicates — through action rather than words — that people and performance matter.

Done well, an award night becomes a story that employees carry with them for their entire career. Done poorly, it becomes an awkward formality that people sit through rather than feel.

The Theme — Your Foundation

Every outstanding award night begins with a clear, cohesive theme that informs every decision from the invitation to the stage design to the trophy style. Popular themes BL Events has executed include "Black Tie Gala" for senior leadership recognition, "Stars of the Year" with an astronomy motif featuring deep navy and gold throughout, and "The Excellence Summit" with a mountain-peak visual identity representing teams reaching new heights.

Stage and AV — Where Dignity Is Created or Destroyed

The stage is where your employees will walk to receive their award — possibly one of the most proud moments of their professional year. The stage setup must be worthy of that moment. A well-designed stage with proper lighting, clear sight lines from every table, a professional LED backdrop, and impeccable microphone quality makes recipients feel genuinely celebrated. Poor lighting or crackly audio reduces what should be a peak moment to an afterthought.

The Awards Themselves

Custom trophies and awards that genuinely reflect the recognition category create far more meaning than generic plaques. We help clients create bespoke awards — laser-engraved crystal, hand-crafted wood and metal combinations, or personalized glass pieces — that recipients genuinely display rather than put in a drawer. Each award should have the recipient's name, the award category, and the year — these details matter enormously to the person receiving it.

Entertainment — Complementary, Not Competing

Entertainment at an award night must complement the evening's purpose, not overshadow it. A brief stand-up comedy set between award categories keeps energy high. A live band during the dinner service creates ambience. A well-chosen emcee who knows the company culture makes transitions seamless and keeps the audience engaged. Avoid anything that runs long, requires audience participation, or dominates more time than the awards themselves.

BL Events Insight

The most powerful element of any award night is the citation — the spoken or video introduction before each award is presented. A well-crafted, specific, genuinely personal citation that mentions the recipient's specific contributions creates a moment of real emotion. We help clients write these, and the difference it makes is remarkable.

How to Plan a Premium Event Without Blowing Your Budget in Gujarat

One of the most persistent myths in event planning is that a premium experience requires a premium-sized budget. This is simply not true — and our decade of working across Gujarat's incredibly diverse wedding and corporate events market has proven it repeatedly. What creates a premium event is not the amount spent, but where it is spent, and how effectively every rupee is used to create impact.

The High-Impact Investment Principle

Every event has elements that guests notice, remember, and talk about — and elements they experience passively without registering. Smart budget allocation means putting money into the first category and reducing spend in the second. The entrance experience, the stage or ceremony setup, the food quality, and one or two signature moments (a spectacular cake, a live artist performance, a stunning sendoff) are almost always remembered. The colour of the chair sashes, the specific brand of mineral water on tables, and the exact number of flower types in the centrepieces are almost never remembered.

Where to Spend — And Where to Save

↑
SPEND on Photography. Photographs and video are the only permanent record of your event. This is not where to economize. A skilled photographer captures what actually happened; an average one captures what was there.
↑
SPEND on the Main Stage or Ceremony Area. This is what every guest looks at all evening and what backgrounds every photograph. One magnificent centrepiece installation here is worth more than décor spread thinly everywhere.
↑
SPEND on Food Quality. Guests will remember exactly how good or average the food was. Reducing quantity before reducing quality when on a tight budget.
↓
SAVE on generic floral fillers. One dramatic flower arrangement is more impactful than five average ones. Greenery and local seasonal blooms are beautiful and cost a fraction of imported flowers.
↓
SAVE on event stationery. Digital invitations have become fully acceptable and save significantly. Printed menu cards and programmes rarely get taken home.
↓
SAVE on the number of courses, not the quality. A three-course dinner of exceptional quality is more impressive than a five-course dinner where two courses disappoint.
Budget is not about how much you have. It's about how clearly you understand what matters most to you and your guests — and having the discipline to spend there first.
BL Events Insight

Always build a 10–15% contingency into your event budget before you begin planning. Not because things will go wrong, but because once the event starts taking shape, you will always find one or two elements you want to enhance. Having that buffer means you enhance them without stress, not with guilt.

Photography Guide: How to Capture Timeless Moments at Any Event

Great event photography is one of the most valuable investments you can make for any celebration. The flowers will wilt, the food will be eaten, the guests will go home — but the photographs remain. They are the permanent record of how a moment actually felt, and they are what you return to again and again in the years that follow.

The Shot List — Your Photographer's North Star

Every event requires a shot list — a specific, prioritised list of must-have photographs shared with your photographer before the event. For a wedding, this includes specific family combination portraits, key ceremony moments, and reception highlights. For a corporate event, this means brand elements, key speakers, award moments, and candid networking shots. A shot list doesn't constrain a good photographer — it ensures the moments you care about are never missed while still leaving room for the unexpected magic.

Lighting Is Everything

Amateur photographers worry about composition. Professional photographers worry about light. The difference between a good event photograph and a great one is almost always lighting. At BL Events, we coordinate with our photography team during venue setup to ensure the lighting design serves both the ambience and the photography simultaneously. This means avoiding harsh backlights behind ceremony stages, ensuring key moments happen in well-lit areas, and discussing the use of additional photography lighting for indoor receptions.

Candid Is King

The posed photographs are important, but the candid moments are what make an album truly alive. The grandmother covering her mouth in surprise. The groom's eyes filling as the bride enters. The children falling asleep under the head table. A skilled event photographer knows to be simultaneously invisible and present — moving through the event without disrupting it, anticipating moments before they happen.

The Details Matter

Before guests arrive, a thorough detail shoot — table settings, centrepieces, invitation suite, the ceremony setup, the couple's accessories — creates a visual record of all the elements you spent months selecting and arranging. These detail photographs anchor the story of the album and give it depth and texture beyond the human moments.

BL Events Insight

When hiring an event photographer, ask to see three or four complete event galleries — not just a portfolio of their best shots. A best-of portfolio shows their ceiling. A complete gallery shows their floor — and that's what your event album will look like.

Cinematic Wedding Films vs Traditional Videos — Which Is Right for You?

One of the most common decisions couples face in wedding videography is choosing between a cinematic film and a traditional video. These are fundamentally different products with different purposes, different editing styles, and different viewing experiences. Understanding the difference is essential before you invest.

What Is a Cinematic Wedding Film?

A cinematic film is a short-form, story-driven production that captures the emotional essence of your wedding day. Typically 3–8 minutes long, it uses carefully selected music, colour grading, slow-motion footage, aerial drone shots, and narrative editing to create something that feels like a movie trailer for your love story. It is designed to be watched again and again, shared on social media, and treasured as a piece of art. What it doesn't include is the full speech, the entire ceremony, or every moment of the evening.

What Is a Traditional Wedding Video?

A traditional video is a comprehensive documentary of your wedding day. It captures the full ceremony with complete audio, the complete speeches, the key dances, the complete dinner reception. A traditional video might be 2–4 hours long and is designed for families and the couple to revisit the complete experience of the day. Parents who want to hear every word of the ceremony and vows — traditional video. Couple who want something beautifully edited to share on Instagram — cinematic film.

Why BL Events Recommends Both

The ideal solution, which we increasingly recommend, is commissioning both a cinematic highlight film and a documentary edit of key moments like the ceremony and speeches. This gives you the emotional piece to share widely and revisit in short bursts, and the comprehensive record to watch on anniversaries or with grandchildren decades from now. The additional cost for both formats from a single production team is typically 25–40% more than either format alone — and almost always worth it.

BL Events Insight

Ask your videographer to show you examples of both formats from the same event. This is the best way to understand the dramatic difference in feel and purpose. Also ask: who specifically will be editing our film? In larger production companies, the editor and the shooter are different people — and the editor shapes the final product as much as anyone.

Behind the Scenes: How We Planned a 500-Guest Wedding in Rajkot in Just 45 Days

The call came on a Thursday afternoon in February. A family in Rajkot had experienced an unavoidable situation — their original event management team had pulled out due to an internal issue, and their daughter's wedding was 45 days away. 500 guests. A venue already booked. A bride who had been dreaming of this day for years. And a family in complete panic.

We said yes within 24 hours of that first call. Here is exactly what the next 45 days looked like.

Days 1–5: Emergency Assessment

The first thing we did was not begin planning. The first thing we did was listen — and document. We met with the family for an intensive three-hour session to understand what was already decided, what was already booked, what needed to be rebooked, and what had never been planned at all. The venue was confirmed. The catering company had a verbal agreement but no signed contract. The photography team was in place. The décor — the most complex element — had been barely discussed.

We also assessed what the family wanted versus what was realistic in 45 days. This honest conversation is one of the most valuable things we do. Telling a family what they want to hear in week one and disappointing them on the day is a failure. Managing expectations transparently from day one — and then exceeding them — is how we define success.

Days 6–15: Vendor Sprint

Our network proved its value in these ten days. We activated relationships with our top-tier decorator — who rearranged their calendar because of our long relationship — confirmed the caterer with a formal contract and a tasting session, locked in a backup tent house for weather contingency, booked a live dhol and shehnai ensemble for the ceremony, and confirmed a professional MC for the reception evening.

In parallel, our décor team held two design sessions with the family to define the wedding's visual identity. The brief became clear: traditional at the ceremony, grand and glamorous at the reception. We proposed a colour story of deep burgundy, ivory, and gold — elegant enough for the reception, warm enough for the ceremony. The family approved within 24 hours.

Days 16–35: Execution Phase

Our team created a master event document — a 47-page comprehensive guide covering every vendor, every timeline point, every contingency plan, every guest seating arrangement, every décor placement diagram, and every contact number. This document was the single source of truth for every team member and every vendor involved in the event.

Mock setup sessions at the venue revealed two important issues: the main stage dimensions were slightly different from what the decorator had planned for, and the electrical load capacity was lower than the lighting team's requirement. Both were resolved with 20 days to spare. Finding these issues during a mock setup — not on the wedding day — is exactly why we do them.

The Wedding Day

Our team was at the venue at 6:00 AM. The ceremony began at 11:00 AM and unfolded exactly as planned. The reception began at 7:30 PM. Guests at the reception never noticed.

How to Organize a Large-Scale Navratri Event in Gujarat — Complete Guide

Navratri is not just a festival in Gujarat — it is a cultural identity. For nine nights, the entire state transforms into a celebration of dance, devotion, colour, and community. For event management companies like BL Events, Navratri represents some of the most complex and rewarding work of the entire year.

Organizing a community Navratri event — whether for a housing society, a business district, a municipality, or a corporate organization — requires a completely different skill set from wedding or corporate event management. The scale, the audience diversity, the cultural sensitivity required, and the sheer energy of the event create unique planning demands.

6 Months Before: Permissions and Ground Work

Large Navratri events require permissions from multiple government bodies — local municipality approval for the venue, noise permission specifying hours and decibel limits, police NOC for crowd management, fire safety certification, and sometimes electrical load permissions. In Gujarat, these permissions can take 4–8 weeks to process. Beginning this process 6 months before the event ensures you have time to address any queries without approaching the event with incomplete approvals.

Venue and Ground Setup

The Navratri ground layout is one of the most important decisions. The traditional concentric circle design for garba requires careful calculation of space per dancer to avoid dangerous crowding. Standard guidelines suggest 2–3 square feet per dancer for comfortable movement. A 10,000-person event therefore needs a garba area of approximately 25,000–30,000 square feet just for the dance space — before accounting for the stage, the deity installation, food stalls, first aid areas, and entry/exit corridors.

Artist Selection and Management

The Navratri anchor — the garba and aarti singer who leads the nine nights — is the most critical booking decision you will make. In Gujarat, the most popular Navratri artists book out 8–12 months in advance. BL Events' celebrity management division handles artist sourcing, contract negotiation, rider requirements, travel and accommodation arrangements, and on-night coordination for all nine artists across the nine nights.

Sound, Lighting, and Stage

Navratri sound requirements are unique. The sound must fill a large outdoor space while remaining clear and crisp for the traditional garba compositions. Stage lighting serves both the performer and the garba dancers — the traditional lighting in fast-cycling colours creates the iconic Navratri atmosphere while ensuring the stage is clearly visible from across the venue. LED truss lighting, laser systems, and synchronized effects are now standard at well-organized Navratri events.

Crowd Safety — Non-Negotiable

The most important planning element of any large Navratri event is crowd safety. BL Events deploys a detailed crowd management plan for every large-scale cultural event we organize — designated entry and exit zones with staggered timing, first aid stations every 200 metres, a command centre with real-time crowd density monitoring, trained ground staff in identifiable uniforms, and clear emergency evacuation routes. No event is worth compromising on these basics.

Navratri is Gujarat's gift to the world — a nine-night festival of pure joy, devotion, and community. Organizing it well is an honour that we approach with deep respect for the culture it celebrates.
BL Events Insight

The most common mistake we see in self-organized Navratri events is underestimating crowd growth from night one to night nine. Attendance almost always grows each night as word spreads in the community. Plan for your maximum expected night-nine crowd from the very beginning of your safety and logistics planning.
